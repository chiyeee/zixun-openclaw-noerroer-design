About Henk Nie

Henk Nie is the CEO and Co-Founder of Nexscope, with over a decade of experience at the intersection of ecommerce, artificial intelligence, and enterprise technology. Before founding Nexscope, Henk led product and engineering teams at multiple high-growth startups in the cross-border ecommerce space, where he developed a deep understanding of the challenges online sellers face at every stage of growth. A firm believer that AI should simplify — not complicate — the seller workflow, Henk built Nexscope around the principle of turning complex market data into clear, actionable intelligence. His writing covers product strategy, market analysis, and the future of AI-driven commerce. When he's not building product, you'll find him analyzing emerging market trends or mentoring early-stage ecommerce founders.
